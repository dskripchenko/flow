Notations: `NOTATIONS.epc`, `.dfd` and `.idef0` — node types, palettes and directions for event-driven process chains, data flow diagrams and IDEF0 function models (`notationRegistry(name)` builds the registry); new outlines `hexagon`, `ellipse`, `operator` (with a `glyph`), `store`, `entity` and `document`; `numbered` types show `data.number`. The layout places a node that only feeds (or only takes from) a port across the flow above or below its node, so IDEF0 controls and mechanisms sit over and under their box.
